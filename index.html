BridgeFund · 2023—current

Building a Scalable FinTech
from the Ground Up.

When I joined BridgeFund, technology delivery relied on an outsourced team of around 25 developers in Sri Lanka. We had no internal product, engineering or data teams. The business had ambitious growth plans, but I recognised that our delivery model would not support the scale we wanted to reach.

I led the shift from outsourcing to building those capabilities in-house, growing a product, engineering and data organisation of more than 65 people from the ground up. The question guiding that change was simple: where could our own technology give BridgeFund a distinctive advantage and drive growth?

That question shaped our build-versus-buy decisions. We integrated Mambu as our core banking system and HubSpot as our main CRM. Buying proven platforms for these capabilities freed our teams to focus on the technology that could differentiate BridgeFund: credit risk models and the experience of our customers.

From that foundation, our data team built machine learning models for credit risk, while product and engineering built our onboarding flow and the My BridgeFund customer environment. Together, these teams connected better risk insight with a simpler customer journey. We moved from relying on outsourced development to owning the capabilities that could help the business scale: understanding risk, improving access to funding and creating lasting customer value.

The next step was to evolve into an agentic engineering team, using AI agents to deliver features faster while maintaining control over quality and releases. This capability helped BridgeFund turn a loss into a €17 million profit in 2026. For me, that is what technology leadership should deliver: stronger teams, better customer experiences and a measurable contribution to business performance. With the people, platforms and delivery capabilities now in place, BridgeFund has a foundation for its next stage of growth.
